# Code of conduct

Framecast is built in the open, and everyone taking part should feel welcome.

**Be kind and respectful.** Assume good intent, disagree about ideas rather than people, and keep feedback specific and constructive.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or sexualised content.

**Where this applies:** issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

**Reporting:** if someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through [x.com/rishit30g](https://x.com/rishit30g). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or block people who don't follow this code.
