# My editing style

Claude reads this before editing any of my Framecast recordings. It is plain language: change
anything, delete what you don't care about, add your own rules. Nothing here is code.

## Pacing
- Remove pauses longer than 0.8 seconds, leaving a short natural gap.
- If I say the same sentence twice, keep the last complete take.
- Cut "um", false starts and dead air at the very start and end.
- Do not speed anything up unless I ask.

## Zooms
- Keep automatic zoom on clicks switched on.
- Add a manual zoom only when I am clearly pointing at something small.

## Captions and text
- Turn captions on.
- No title text unless I ask for one.

## Look
- Background: Sonoma Horizon. Shape: Auto.
- Leave padding, corners and shadow as they are.

## Camera
- Leave the camera where it is.

## Start and end
- Fade in at the start and fade out at the end.

## Private things
- If you can see passwords, API keys, email addresses or phone numbers on screen, blur them and
  tell me where.

## Export
- Do not export until I say the edit looks right. When I do: 1080p, 60 fps.
